The rule, and where β came from

S = cumulative steps, so dS/dt is cadence

if dS/dt < α  movement cannot explain it
and dHR/dt > β  but heart rate is climbing
→ stress score ∝ dHR/dt

β = __BETA__ bpm/min. Measured, not chosen. The real recording's own dHR/dt floor is __NF_MED__ ± __NF_SIG__ bpm/min using a median-absolute-deviation σ, which ignores the genuine climbs instead of being inflated by them. β sits at __BSIG__σ.

__ARTIFACTS__ samples were dropped before any of this. The optical sensor locked onto the second harmonic four times and reported exactly double — 70 → 136 bpm inside one 4-second sample. Left in, the largest “stress onset” in the file would be a glitch.

__ABSTAIN__% of the real record is abstained on. Dropouts under 30 s are bridged; longer ones are not, and the detector declines to guess a slope across missing data.
